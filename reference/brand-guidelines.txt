Our brand guidelines
Our brand guidelines set the tone for our Café’s unique visual identity, reflecting the vibrant
spirit and commitment to excellence that define us. These guidelines are integral to every piece
of communication, whether in print or digital form. They outline the precise use of our distinctive
logo, our signature colors, our carefully chosen fonts, and our authentic messaging.
By adhering to these brand guidelines, we ensure that every marketing and communication
effort not only accurately represents our COMPANY but also embodies our brand's core values
and vision.
--- page 3 ---
Logo
The company logo should be prominent and visible in all visual communications.
It should not be placed with another graphic or edited to create a new image.
For legibility, keep the area around the logo clear.
The cafe logo colors can not be changed or modified. Use only the provided official images.
this logo is the representation of our cafe and our values that drive the visual consistency between all our locations and design
principles.
